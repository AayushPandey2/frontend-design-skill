# /formforge-flagship

## Purpose & Scope

Architect and build ambitious, sophisticated multi-view or motion-rich frontend experiences with signature craft, choreography, and rigorous QA.

## Activation Criteria & Boundaries

Use for flagship launches, interactive storytelling, bespoke portfolios, or complex stateful apps. Requires strict economy so ambition never becomes bloat (<= 5 passes). Do not auto-chain or circularly invoke sibling skills; suggest optional next skills only after completing this workflow.

## Workflow

1. Establish product positioning, creative direction, and architectural/motion plan.
2. Selectively load only the specialized typography, surface, motion, framework, and perf modules needed.
3. Build modularly with native-first motion (`motion-and-interactions.md`), reduced-motion parity, and WCAG 2.2 AA compliance.
4. Conduct multi-pass rendered visual, motion, responsive, and performance verification (<= 5 passes).
